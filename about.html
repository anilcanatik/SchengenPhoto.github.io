About SchengenPhoto

At SchengenPhoto, our mission is to make the process of preparing Schengen Visa photos as simple and accessible as possible.

Our platform prioritizes your privacy by ensuring that all image processing is done locally on your device. This means your photos never leave your computer, giving you peace of mind that your personal data remains secure.

We are proud to offer our services completely free of charge, with no hidden fees or premium features, only through the Google Ads service used on the website. SchengenPhoto is dedicated to helping travelers meet their visa photo requirements without any hassle.
